**English:** A. The total number of writes is 1024.
B. Since the cache can hold only 1/2 of the array, the first part is loaded into the cache and then replaced by the second part, so elements in different rows of each column must be reloaded into the cache. But within an array element, `c` misses while `m`, `y`, and `k` hit, so there is 1/4 miss, giving 256 misses.
